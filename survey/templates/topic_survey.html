Question Topic Survey

Report Description

This experiment focus on question diversity, topic coverage, and look and feel of machine generated instructions.

Rule-set
  • From our dataset of over 2900+ questions, we've identified 10 distinct topics and their frequency. We've also provided representative words for each topic and examples of questions that fall within each category. Additionally, we've asked 4 questions and assigned a unique number to each one.

  • 1. Humanness. The questions should resemble those expected from a real person.

  • 2. Topic Coverage. A wide range of topics should be covered, ideally including the top 5-10 topics.

  • 3. Engagement. The questions should keep the subject matter expert motivated during the information discovery phase.

  • 4. Novelty. The system should help identify any previously unconsidered topics or questions.

  • No datetime information or location/IP data is being collected.

Score the quality of the questions on a scale of 1 to 3, with 3 being good, 2 being moderate, and 1 being poor
